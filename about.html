About Me

Hi, I'm Liam Gillies, a creative professional passionate about storytelling through visual media. With a background in design and filmmaking, I specialize in crafting compelling narratives that engage and inspire audiences.

My work spans motion graphics, video production, and interactive experiences. I believe in the power of visuals to communicate complex ideas simply and beautifully.

When I'm not creating, you can find me exploring new technologies, hiking in nature, or experimenting with photography. I'm always excited to collaborate on projects that push creative boundaries.
